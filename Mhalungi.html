Mhalungi

Botanical Name: Citrus medica (L)

Common Name: Mhalungi

Family: Convolvulaceae

Description

A green plant with broad, oval leaves and a climbing habit.

Medicinal Uses

  • Traditionally used in Ayurveda and folk medicine for various ailments.
  • Traditionally used in the management of diabetes.
  • Traditionally used for skin cuts and wounds.
